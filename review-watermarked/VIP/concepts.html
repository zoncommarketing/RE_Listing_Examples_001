Adaptive reuse

A character-rich platform for phased hospitality and destination reuse

A phased reuse opportunity: open the existing gathering and food-service spaces first, test demand, then consider lodging or other expansion after approvals, infrastructure, cost and return analysis.

  • Buyer brief Word
  • Flyer: the property collection PDF
  • Flyer: the future vision PDF
  • Concept packet: renderings, current photographs and the buyer brief ZIP
Who it could suit
  • Owner-developers and operator-investors
  • Boutique hospitality and event investors
  • Adaptive-reuse specialists
  • Family offices or local investors partnering with an experienced operator
What the property brings
  • An existing character-rich building gives any new concept an identity to build on
  • Several indoor and outdoor environments can support phased activation
  • Commercial kitchen and event infrastructure may shorten selected operating pathways
  • Acreage creates optionality, but only after boundary and entitlement verification
A possible operating program
  • Phase one: refreshed event, dining, meeting, or day-retreat operations
  • Phase two: boutique hospitality services and enhanced guest commons
  • Phase three: approved lodging, wellness, or complementary structures if feasibility supports them
  • Use partnerships and temporary programming to test demand before heavy capital deployment
Improvement priorities
  • Complete title, survey, zoning, building, environmental, utility, and market diligence
  • Resolve the discrepancy between the 4,228-square-foot floor-plan total and approximately 6,000 square feet in current marketing
  • Prepare concept-level code, infrastructure, parking, and cost studies for each phase
  • Select an experienced operator and build conservative sources-and-uses, stabilization, and exit cases
Questions to explore
  • Which phase can generate revenue with the least entitlement and capital risk?
  • Who will operate the property and what relevant experience do they bring?
  • How will building area, equipment, parcel boundaries, and legal uses be verified?
  • What return threshold and contingency reserve does the investment require?
  • Which expansion ideas remain viable after parking, septic, fire, accessibility, and cost analysis?
