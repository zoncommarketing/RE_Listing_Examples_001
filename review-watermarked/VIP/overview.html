Overview · 8595 US Highway 51, Cobden, Illinois

Executive summary

A lodge on the hill, an amphitheater on the lawn, and Southern Illinois acreage to build a business on.

Built in 2009 as a destination for gathering, 8595 US Highway 51 pairs a timber-and-stone great hall and a working commercial kitchen with something rarer: a terraced lawn that steps down to a covered stage above a pond. Every figure is sourced and dated on the Facts page.

  • Great hall with cathedral ceiling and loft; two stone fireplaces (great hall and dining room)
  • Commercial kitchen and two service bars
  • Terraced-lawn amphitheater with covered stage
  • Pond, three levels of decks, patio and pergola
  • US 51 frontage inside the Shawnee Hills
