Highlights

What sets it apart.

  1. 01

    A lodge built for gatherings

    A 40′9″ × 30′5″ great hall under a cathedral pine ceiling, ringed by an iron-railed loft, with a stone fireplace at one end and a stone-fronted bar at the other.

  2. 02

    An amphitheater cut into the hill

    Mown terraces step from the decks down to a covered stage, with the pond as the backdrop.

  3. 03

    A working commercial kitchen

    Hood line with range and convection ovens, refrigerated prep, three-compartment sinks, commercial dishwasher and a walk-in cooler — everything a food-service operator needs from day one.

  4. 04

    A wall that opens

    The 468 sq ft enclosed porch has glass overhead doors along two sides. Down, it is a three-season room; up, it is part of the deck.

  5. 05

    Three levels of outdoor room

    Covered upper deck, wraparound main deck and a lower paver patio with fire pit and pergola, all facing the lawn and water.

  6. 06

    Highway frontage, rural setting

    Direct access from US 51 (5,800 vehicles a day, IDOT 2025) on acreage inside the Shawnee Hills, with Giant City State Park and the wine trail as neighbors.
